Vestigo

A personal movie and TV tracker for iOS. Discover what to watch next, track what you've seen, and get recommendations that reflect your history.

Discovery

Trending & upcoming — trending, popular, new releases, and upcoming titles.

For You — personalized recommendations based on your watch history and ratings.

Pick For Me — answer a few questions and get results tailored to your mood, powered by TMDb and a unique archetype system.

On every title

Cast & crew — full credits with person profiles and filmographies.

Streaming availability — prices and direct links to streaming apps where available.

Trailers — watch trailers without leaving the app.

Search

Find exactly what you're in the mood for.

Find anything — search titles or people by name.

Genre browsing — collections for every genre and franchise, right from the empty state.

Filters — narrow results by runtime, release date, and minimum rating.

Watchlist

Everything you've saved, and everything you've seen.

Watched & watchlist — mark movies and series as watched or save them for later.

Ratings — rate what you've seen from 1–5, with half-star increments.

Episode progress — track season and episode progress for TV series.

Not interested — hide items you don't want to see again.

Collections

Custom collections — group titles any way you like.

Automatic grouping — franchise and series collections are suggested automatically as you log more titles.

Your library and preferences are stored on-device and in iCloud where enabled.

Vestigo was vibe coded: AI assisted with code implementation, while the product thinking, decisions, review, and non-coding work were all done by people.

Vestigo is not affiliated with TMDB, IMDb, OMDb, TheTVDB, Watchmode, YouTube, Wikimedia, or their parent companies.

Vestigo combines catalog, ratings, recommendations, availability, trailer, and open-knowledge data from the following services:

TMDB This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Watchmode Streaming availability and provider data are provided in part by Watchmode. TheTVDB Series, season, episode, and franchise metadata are provided in part by TheTVDB. OMDb This product uses the OMDb API but is not endorsed or certified by OMDb or IMDb. Ratings and movie data are provided in part by The Open Movie Database and IMDb. YouTube Trailer playback uses embedded YouTube videos where available. AMC Theatres Cinema showtimes and theatre locations are provided by AMC Theatres. Wikidata and Wikipedia Original-media and knowledge links use Wikimedia projects and their content licenses.
