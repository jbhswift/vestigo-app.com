Your library and preferences are stored on-device and in iCloud where enabled.

Vestigo was vibe coded: AI assisted with code implementation, while the product thinking, decisions, review, and non-coding work were all done by people.

Vestigo is not affiliated with TMDB, IMDb, OMDb, TheTVDB, Watchmode, YouTube, Wikimedia, or their parent companies.

Vestigo combines catalog, ratings, recommendations, availability, trailer, and open-knowledge data from the following services:

TMDB This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Watchmode Streaming availability and provider data are provided in part by Watchmode. TheTVDB Series, season, episode, and franchise metadata are provided in part by TheTVDB. OMDb This product uses the OMDb API but is not endorsed or certified by OMDb or IMDb. Ratings and movie data are provided in part by The Open Movie Database and IMDb. YouTube Trailer playback uses embedded YouTube videos where available. AMC Theatres Cinema showtimes and theatre locations are provided by AMC Theatres. Wikidata and Wikipedia Original-media and knowledge links use Wikimedia projects and their content licenses.
