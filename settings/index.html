Settings

Fine-tune how Vestigo looks, what it shows, and how your data behaves — all from the app.

Streaming services — choose which services you subscribe to; availability results are pinned to the top accordingly.

IMDb or TMDb ratings — switch rating sources if you've added your own OMDb API key.

Content filters — prioritise English, hide explicit results, hide anime, reduce kids & family content, hide watched items, hide short films, hide extras & promos, hide upcoming releases.

Watchlist behaviour — automatically remove watched items from your watchlist, or keep them in a separate "Watched" section.

Rating prompts — turn off the prompt to rate something right after marking it watched.

Watch dates — track dates automatically, or set them manually.

Default filters — set the default Movie/TV/Both filter for each tab.

Light & dark mode — switch appearance, or follow your system setting.

Accent & background colour — personalize the app's colour, like the one behind this very page.

Carousel order — reorder the Home tab's carousels, including the For You recommendations sub-carousels.

Export & import — back up your watched history as a .csv or .txt file, or import one from Letterboxd.

Hidden items — review titles you've marked "not interested" or "never show again."

Replay tour — revisit the first-launch welcome tour anytime.

Clear caches — free up storage from temporary data (ratings, streaming availability, search results) without touching your watchlist, watched history, ratings, or collections.

Reset — reset settings and data entirely, if you want a clean start.
